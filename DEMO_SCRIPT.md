# Google AI Day demo script

## 20-second opening

“A founder often receives an agreement when the business is already in a hurry. The first problem is not replacing a lawyer; it is understanding what the company is accepting and what needs escalation. Pactrix Review turns an agreement into a traceable decision brief.”

## Live flow

1. Upload `pactrix_synthetic_vendor_agreement.pdf`.
2. Show parties, money, dates, payment terms, obligations, renewal, termination, liability, IP, data handling, jurisdiction, and evidence pages.
3. Show deterministic findings for unilateral modification and automatic renewal.
4. Show the practical next-action list.
5. Explain that review coverage is workflow coverage, not a legal-risk score.
6. Upload `hindi_scan.pdf` and show `Human verification required`.

## 20-second vision bridge

“This is the first narrow product from Pactrix Labs. If businesses repeatedly use a trusted, evidence-grounded workflow before and after signature, Pactrix can expand from review into obligations, negotiation history, policy checks, and eventually the intelligence infrastructure around legal transactions in India.”

## Honest close

“Today we are testing extraction quality, grounding, and refusal—not claiming legal accuracy or production readiness. The next proof is five design partners and a labelled evaluation set.”
